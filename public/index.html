Education & learning

Tools for how people learn: sensors that make science visible in class, microlearning that fits a working day, AI that writes Scratch for children, and design-thinking programmes in Sri Lanka.

Much of this is team work. I contribute to SPARK at NUS CHILL and to Kiwrious at the Augmented Human Lab, I co-own Kiwrious and the ICE programme sites, and Scratch GPT is mine.
